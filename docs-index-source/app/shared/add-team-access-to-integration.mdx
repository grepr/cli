When you have teams in your Grepr organization, you can grant access to the integration to one or more of those teams. To configure team access when you create or edit an integration:

1. In the **Grant Team Access** section, click the plus sign icon (+).
1. Select the team or teams that should be granted access to the integration.
1. Click **Create**.

   ![The Grant Team Access to a resource card in the Grepr UI.](/images/shared/add-team-access-to-integration.png)

To learn more, see [Manage teams in the Grepr platform](/admin/manage-teams) and [Permissions in the Grepr platform](/grepr-platform/permissions).
